Compute annual joint demographic fate probabilities.

Implements the paper's competing-risk model using:

  1. analytic annual survival;
  2. positive one-dimensional quadrature of non-hunting death while hunting is active;
  3. analytic non-hunting death after hunting;
  4. total hunting as the remaining annual probability mass; and
  5. an exact proportional split of hunting between Sweden and Finland.

The proportional country split is exact because both national hunting hazards have the same temporal shape, E_country * (k - tau), and differ only through their pressure coefficients.

This function preserves the timing convention used by the model: t_birth_to_end_hunt is used as both k and the integration endpoint.

The returned ordering is:

  1. survived the annual interval;
  2. died from non-hunting causes;
  3. hunted in Sweden;
  4. hunted in Finland.

Calculate expected demographic hunting pressure.

Converts a quota into demographic hunting-pressure coefficients. The linearly declining hunting hazard integrates to E * tau_h^2 / 2 over the interval from zero to tau_h, which explains the factor of two and the squared hunting-time parameter.

Returns exact zero pressure when the quota is zero. Demographic groups with zero abundance are permitted and receive exact zero pressure.

NDemographic population composition.
Returns
Hunting-pressure coefficient for each demographic group.

Positive integrand for non-hunting death during hunting.

Unlike an ODE solve, direct one-dimensional quadrature integrates the non-negative hazard contribution itself. This avoids the negative solver overshoot that can occur when hunting pressure is large and almost all of the integral is concentrated immediately after the start of hunting.

The original time coordinate is rescaled before this function is called. If a = E_total * k + mu_m, x = a * tau, and x = x_upper * unit_interval, this function evaluates the remaining dimensionless integrand on [0, 1]. Keeping the numerical integral away from zero prevents integrate_1d from imposing an unattainable absolute error through its relative-error criterion.

theta contains, in order, total hunting pressure, a, and x_upper. The complementary coordinate and data arrays are unused but are required by integrate_1d.

Differential equation for non-hunting death during hunting.

Implements the non-hunting death equation from the competing-risks model:

dD / dtau = mu_m * alive(tau)

where the alive probability follows the analytic solution

alive(tau) = exp( -E_total * (k * tau - tau^2 / 2) -mu_m * tau ).

This scalar ODE is preferable to numerically integrating total hunting, because total hunting approaches one under extreme hunting pressure whereas non-hunting death approaches zero. Total hunting is subsequently obtained as the remaining probability mass, which enforces probability conservation.

Parameters
tauTime since the beginning of the modeled hunting interval.
cumulative_deathCurrent cumulative non-hunting death probability. The derivative does not depend on the current cumulative value.
kHunting-time parameter and integration endpoint.
hunting_pressure_totalSum of Swedish and Finnish hunting pressures.
mu_mInstantaneous non-hunting mortality rate.
Returns
Derivative of cumulative non-hunting death probability.
