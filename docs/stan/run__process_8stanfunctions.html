For each demographic group, Equation (3) in Vanko et al (2026) gives the conditional expected fate counts as

\[ \mathbb{E}[\mathbf{u}_{a,s,t}^{1}\mid n_{a,s,t},\boldsymbol{\rho}_{a,s,t}] =n_{a,s,t}\boldsymbol{\rho}_{a,s,t} \]

The annual loop obtains probabilities from compute_fate_probabilities, multiplies them by population counts, and calls allocate_fates for the continuous approximation described in Section 2.4

Columns follow the paper order: survival, Swedish hunting, Finnish hunting, other death or bycatch

The fitted survivor boundary is integerized once. Each forecast year uses integer ageing, binomial births and sex allocation, one joint competing- risks ODE per non-empty demographic group, and multinomial fate sampling.

Initialization converts fractional fitted survivors to integers by drawing a Poisson total and allocating it using the fitted demographic proportions This conversion happens before the yearly loop and is not repeated each year Zero totals remain zero without a multinomial draw
